The App – No Data Collection

This app collects no user data. All data is stored locally on your device.

Bluetooth Connection (optional)

If you optionally connect your IQOS device via Bluetooth, we use this interface solely to read device data (e.g., battery status, MAC address).

  • No Server Transmission: All data is processed exclusively locally on your smartphone. No data is transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • Legal Basis & Revocation: Processing is based on your consent Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO) granted via the app permission. You can revoke this permission at any time in your smartphone's system settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

We follow a strict 'Privacy by Design' approach. Since all your entries and usage data (such as logged smoking sessions including timestamps and costs, personal app settings, and connected devices) are stored exclusively local within the app's secure sandbox database on your own smartphone, we never have access to this data.

The data remains on your device for as long as you use the app. You have full control over the retention period: you can delete individual entries or reset settings at any time directly within the app. Uninstalling the app from your smartphone will automatically and permanently delete all locally stored data from your device. No server-side archiving or backup storage is performed by us.

Contacting us by Email

If you contact us by email (e.g., for support inquiries), your email address and the data contained in the email will be stored by us in order to process your request.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO).
  • The data will be deleted as soon as your request has been finally resolved and there are no statutory retention obligations to the contrary.

Domain Infrastructure and Web Hosting (IONOS & GitHub)

For the technical operation of this website, we use services for the provision of the domain and web space hosting.

  • Domain & DNS: The domain of this website is registered with IONOS SE. When the domain is accessed, requests are routed through the nameservers of IONOS SE to establish the connection to the website.
  • Web hosting: The actual content of this website is provided via GitHub Pages by the provider GitHub Inc. When accessing the page, GitHub automatically processes technical data (e.g., IP address, browser type, operating system, time of access) in server logs.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO).
  • Third-country transfer: GitHub Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which ensures an adequate level of data protection.
  • No cookies are set on this website and no external resources are loaded.

Rights of Users

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing of your personal data, as well as the right to data portability. You also have the right to object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, at any time to the processing of data concerning you which is carried out on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO). Since the app itself does not collect any data, in practice these rights primarily concern data generated by the website infrastructure (server logs) or via email contact. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
